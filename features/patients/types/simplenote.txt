At the moment, our Patient interface contains only demographic information. These summary metrics—such 
as visit count, active medications, allergies, and outstanding balance—belong to different bounded contexts
 in your HMS (Visits, Pharmacy, Billing, etc.). Mixing them into the Patient entity would tightly couple 
 those domains.

Instead, I recommend creating a separate PatientWorkspaceSummary type that aggregates these values 
specifically for the workspace. Then patient-workspace.tsx can fetch both:

patient → demographics and identifiers
summary → cross-module statistics

and pass them independently:
<PatientHeader patient={patient} />
<PatientSummaryCards summary={summary} />

This keeps your DDD boundaries clean while still giving the UI everything it needs. 
It will also scale much better as you add modules like Laboratory, Radiology, Pharmacy, Billing, and Admissions.
